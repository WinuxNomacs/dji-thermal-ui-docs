Exports¶

Export… opens the Export dialog:

  • on the frame page, for the frame shown, as converted or with the parameters changed;
  • on the flight page's Files tab, for the frames the filter shows or all of them, measured with one of the flight's datasets or the frames' own values, with a palette and one range shared by all of them;
  • on the viewer, for the mosaic tab shown.

The formats¶

A frame, the frames chosen or a whole flight export as:

  • Original: the files as they are.
  • TIFF: the temperatures.
  • PNG: coloured as the view, one pixel per sensor pixel.
  • R-JPEG: DJI's format, with the measurement parameters in its header.

A mosaic exports as its GeoTIFF, or a PNG of the view at full size.

Each format says what it is for. R-JPEG and Original are turned off, with the reason, for a normalized dataset or replaced values, which only TIFF carries.

Private¶

Each format can also be Private: without the location, the serial numbers and the free-text fields where a name or an email address could be. A private mosaic's GeoTIFF has no place.

Downloads¶

Several files come as a zip, made as it downloads. Export PNG, in the viewer, still saves the view as shown.

Mosaics¶

The viewer shows a dataset's mosaics, opened with View mosaics on its card or from the navigation pane.

The tabs¶

Each tab's tooltip says what it shows.

  • Mosaic: each pixel is one frame's own temperature, as the frames were converted.
  • Harmonized: normalized, with one ambient and reflected temperature for every frame, and each frame's offset against its neighbours removed (the camera's drift between recalibrations), so the seams don't show as bands.
  • Source frame: which frame each pixel comes from (the one that saw it closest to straight down), with a line along each seam between the frames (in the mosaic report too).
  • View angle: how far from straight down each pixel was seen, in degrees.
  • Visual: the visual mosaic, when the dataset has one: the visual camera's images, each placed where its thermal frame is. Pointer info gives the Mosaic tab's temperatures, and a click opens the frames that saw the spot.
  • WebODM and Heights: an orthophoto and a surface model brought back from WebODM.

The viewer opens on Mosaic, or on Harmonized when the frames disagree (their offsets against their neighbours spanning more than 3 °C over the middle 90% of them), with a notice saying so. A link that names a tab opens that one.

Under the mosaic is a street map or satellite imagery, chosen with the switch at the top right of the map (see The maps under the mosaics).

The side panel¶

The panel is grouped under:

  • View: the palette, the temperature range, Image (brightness and contrast, gamma, equalization and sharpening) and Isotherm. They are redrawn as their sliders are dragged. The colour scale is labelled with its range's ends.
  • Values: Pointer info (the values under the cursor), Parameters and This mosaic.
  • Kept: the saved measurements and the findings.
  • About.

On a first visit Image, Parameters, This mosaic and The mosaic start folded, and each section then stays as it was left.

Pointer info¶

Pointer info gives the values under the cursor. A click keeps them and outlines the frame there; Escape or a second click on that frame lets go of it.

From the viewer¶

The viewer's header has Frames (the frames explorer), Compare with… (the compare page), Export PNG (the mosaic as shown, with its saved measurements and colour scale, the basemap left out), Export… (the mosaic whole, at full resolution: see Exports) and Mosaic report (see Reports).

Measuring¶

Spots, lines, areas, boxes and circles are measured on mosaics, in the viewer and on the compare page, and on single frames, on the frame page.

Drawing a shape¶

The drawing tools are on the map's top edge, beside the zoom buttons. A shape drawn is measured in a card on the map, closed with × or Escape. The card gives the temperature at a spot, or a shape's mean, min, max and standard deviation, its surroundings and the ΔT against them, the share inside the isotherm, its pixels, and a line's profile or an area's histogram.

On the card:

  • Save measurement keeps it, listed in the side panel under Kept.
  • Keep as finding, for a spot or an area of a mosaic: see Findings.
  • Source frames, for a spot or an area of a mosaic: see below.

On a frame page, the Thermal/Visual/Both switch shows the frame's visual image in its place or beside it, with the same shapes and the cursor on it, and measures the thermal frame under them.

Saved measurements¶

Saved measurements can be compared, measured again with other settings, and exported as CSV and GeoJSON. Each can have a comment, shown in the viewer's list, in the CSV and GeoJSON, and in the mosaic report.

Source frames¶

The source frames window shows every frame that saw a spot or an area of a mosaic, the one the mosaic used first, each with its time and view angle and the spot marked or the area outlined in it, in the thermal frame or, with the Thermal/Visual switch, its visual image.

It opens from:

  • Source frames on a measurement's card;
  • a spot or an area drawn (or a click) on the Source frame or Visual tab;
  • a saved measurement's;
  • on the compare page, a spot or an area drawn with the tools on a pane's map.

A finding can be kept from it. Mosaics built before version 0.5.0 list only the frames they used: build them again to list every frame.
